Model Derivation — Pukite (2019)
fd = 1 / (Td/2) = 1/13.6061 c/d
fy = 2 / Ty   (semi-annual impulse)

Alias: fCW = fd − 26·fy/2
     Talias = Ty / (Ty/(Td/2) − 26)
        = 365.242 / (26.847 − 26)
        ≈ 432.77 days

Forcing: F(t) = Adrac·|cos(πt/(Td/2) + φd)|
           ·Aann·cos²(πt/Ty + φa/2)
           · (1 + ε·cos(2πt/Tnode + φn))

Resonator: 2nd-order DiffEq, Q≈100
    ẍ + (ω0/Q)ẋ + ω0²x = F(t)
Physical Interpretation
The draconic fortnight (13.606 d = Td/2) is the half-period of the lunar draconic month, driving a biannual equatorial-crossing impulse as the Moon crosses the equatorial plane twice per draconic month.

Stroboscopic aliasing by the annual cycle (semi-annual impulse train) down-converts the draconic forcing to 432.77 days — matching the observed Chandler period exactly. The 13th harmonic of the fortnightly tide falls within the resonant bandwidth of the Earth's nutation at Q ≈ 100.

The 9.3-year nodal envelope (half the 18.6-year lunar nodal precession) modulates the wobble amplitude. The ~6.4-year beat between the 433-day Chandler wobble and the annual wobble is clearly visible in the time series.
